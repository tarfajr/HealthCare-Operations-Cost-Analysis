# AnalystLab Africa – Week 3, Track B (Data Science)
**Hospital Operations: Statistical Investigation & Insight Discovery**

**Research question:** How do total claim costs vary across encounter classes, and is encounter duration (and the presence of procedures) associated with recorded claim cost?

## Contents
- `Week3_TrackB_Healthcare_Operations_Analysis.ipynb` – full reproducible analysis, report, findings and recommendations
- `outputs/` – saved tables (`*.csv`) and 10 figures (`figures/`) generated by the notebook

## How to reproduce
1. Python 3.9+ and `pip install pandas numpy matplotlib seaborn scipy jupyter`
2. Open the notebook; in section 5 replace every `PASTE_PATH_TO/...` with the path to your own CSV file.
3. Kernel -> Restart & Run All (random seed fixed at 42).

## Privacy
Patient names/addresses are never printed or saved. Do not commit the raw CSV files to a public repository.
